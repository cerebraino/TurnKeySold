## Methodology

Prospects are ranked by **ability to pay** (most recent / largest funding round → highest purchase intent). For each domain we ran parallel searches against the eight high-signal surfaces in the Domain Buyer Prospecting Guide:

- Y Combinator Launch YC (`ycombinator.com/launches`)
- Y Combinator Startup Directory (`ycombinator.com/companies`)
- Hacker News Show HN & Who is hiring?
- Product Hunt daily launches
- Indie Hackers
- Crunchbase funding announcements
- Wellfound / AngelList
- LinkedIn founder posts

Contacts marked **bold** were retrieved directly from a tool-fetched source. Other emails follow standard founder-pattern conventions and are flagged.
