# The Three-Line Framework for Selling Premium Domain Names
### A Practical White Paper for AI-Assisted Outbound

**Author:** Juan Aguirre (working methodology)

---

## Executive Summary

Most domain investors try to sell a domain in the first message.

The objective of a cold outbound message is **not to sell the domain**.

The objective is to **earn a reply**.

A reply starts a conversation.
A conversation creates an opportunity.
The sale happens later.

Modern buyers are overloaded with AI-generated outreach. Long messages explaining domain quality, SEO, comparable sales, and pricing are usually ignored.

Short, relevant messages perform better because they respect attention and ask for very little.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Sell the opportunity, not the domain.

Wrong:

> I own NoFail.ai. It is a premium AI domain...

Better:

> Your recent AI reliability announcement made me think of the premium domain NoFail.ai.

The conversation is about **their strategy**, not your inventory.

---

## 2. Start with a real signal

Every message should begin with something true about the recipient.

Examples:

- recently raised funding
- hiring
- launched AI product
- expanding internationally
- entering Hispanic market
- announced new initiative
- rebranding
- acquisition

Avoid generic personalization.

---

## 3. Explain why the signal matters

Bridge the signal to the domain.

Example:

> Saw you're expanding into Hispanic healthcare.

↓

> The premium domain LatinoMedico.com immediately came to mind.

---

## 4. Make one tiny ask

Never ask for a meeting.

Never include pricing.

Never send Calendly.

Instead ask something answerable with one word.

Examples:

- Worth sending a few details?
- Interested?
- Should I send it over?
- Worth a quick look?
- Relevant?

---

## 5. Be explicit that it is a domain

Avoid:

"I own NoFail.ai."

This can be interpreted as owning a company.

Prefer:

- the premium domain NoFail.ai
- the domain NoFail.ai
- the domain name NoFail.ai

Even better:

"The premium domain NoFail.ai immediately came to mind."

This removes yourself from the centre of the message.

---

# Recommended Structure

Line 1
Signal

Line 2
Strategic observation linking the signal to the domain

Line 3
Tiny ask

Nothing else.

No biography.

No company introduction.

No links.

No pricing.

No long explanation.

You are an expert B2B copywriter specialising in premium domain-name outbound.

Objective:
Generate a first-touch cold email or LinkedIn message designed ONLY to obtain a reply.

Rules:

1. Maximum 250 characters (prefer 150–220).
2. Three-line structure.
3. Begin with a real business signal.
4. Connect that signal to the premium domain.
5. Make one tiny ask.
6. Do not ask for a meeting.
7. Do not mention price.
8. Do not include links.
9. Do not explain domain investing.
10. Use "the premium domain" instead of "I own".

# Final Thought

The first message should create curiosity—not close a sale.

If the recipient replies "Sure", "Interested", or "Tell me more", the outreach has already succeeded.

The domain, valuation, comparables, and negotiation belong in the next conversation.
