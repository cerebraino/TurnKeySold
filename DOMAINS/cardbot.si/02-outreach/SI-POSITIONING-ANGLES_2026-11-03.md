- **The sibling field is clean — and that cuts both ways.** `cardbot.com` was registered in 1998 and now serves a **"Domain Available / Premium domain available"** lander, i.e. it is itself on the market. `cardbot.ai` is **unregistered** (WHOIS, 2026-11-03). We say that to the owner and, in softer form, in the copy.

1. **No protection pitch to a company that doesn't own the name.** With `.ai` free and `.com` a lander, there is no "someone is squatting your brand" story here. The angle is **naming**, not defence — writing it as protection would be inventing a threat.
2. **No claim that `.com` is a competitor.** It is a for-sale lander, which is a market signal, not a rival.
3. **No urgency.** Nothing expires, nothing is about to be taken by a named party.
4. **No registry-price economics in first touch** (`~$12 vs ~$90` stays out — pricing-free rule; also EXTERNAL-UNVERIFIED until the owner rules it in for a second touch).
