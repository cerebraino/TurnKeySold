## 1. Domain Valuation Assessment

### Positioning (corrected)
**payauto.ai = "Pay Automatically" + AI.** The category domain for **agentic payments** — AI agents that autonomously issue payments on behalf of users. NOT automotive ("pay for your car"). The double reading of "auto" (automatic / automotive) is now a liability for car messaging and an asset for automation messaging — all outreach must lead with **automatic/autonomous**, never vehicles.

### The Trend (early-stage, moving fast)
- "Agentic payments" = AI agents that can pay for things (book travel, order supplies, manage subscriptions, spend on behalf of users).
- Stripe launched its **Agentic Commerce Suite** ("The new standard for agentic commerce", "Enable programmatic machine payments", "Sell your products and services to agents") — live product page: stripe.com/use-cases/agentic-commerce.
- Visa (Visa Intelligent Commerce) and Mastercard (**Agent Pay**) have both shipped agent-payment products; PayPal is expanding into AI-triggered payments.
- Web3 rails (USDC, agent-payable APIs) emerging via Payman AI, Nevermined, Fetch.ai.
- Concierge "buy for me" AI: Perplexity Shopping, Rabbit R1, AI devices that transact.

## 3. Bundle Strategy (PayAuto.ai + autopaga.com)

- **PayAuto.ai** = English/global flagship: agentic payments infrastructure, agent platforms, crypto rails, concierge AI.
- **autopaga.com** = Spanish/LATAM twin: "auto paga" = "pay automatically" in Spanish — Mercado Pago, Clip, Kushki, dLocal, Nubank.
- **Pair logic:** both names say "pay automatically" in their language; one .ai (global tech) + one .com (LATAM mass-market). Sell as a pair at 1.8× single-domain valuation — the buyer who wants global agentic payments also wants the LATAM on-ramp.
- **Sequence:** approach global leads with payauto.ai first, offer autopaga.com as the LATAM extension; approach LATAM leads with autopaga.com first, offer payauto.ai as the global extension.
