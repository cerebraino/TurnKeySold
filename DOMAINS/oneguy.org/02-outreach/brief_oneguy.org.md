- **Niche:** AI-Enabled Solopreneurship / One-Person Business Movement

## Messaging Strategy
- **Primary Angle:** Movement-Naming. "One Guy" is the identity of the AI-enabled solopreneur. Whoever owns OneGuy.org owns the name of the category. Every message answers: "Your product enables one person to do what used to require a team. OneGuy.org names that movement."
- **Key Talking Points:** .org = community, not commercial. The domain as a user community hub, content platform, or companion brand. Complements existing products — doesn't compete.
- **Phase Approach:** AI-first platforms (Lindy, Artisan) → coding/no-code (Replit, Bubble) → infrastructure (Zapier, Mercury) → community (Indie Hackers, Stripe Atlas).

## Tone & Cultural Considerations
- **Tone:** Aspirational, community-forward, non-commercial. This is not a sales pitch — it's an invitation to own the name of a movement. The .org TLD reinforces "mission, not transaction."
- **Objection — "We don't need a community domain":** "Your users are already building communities around your product on Discord, Reddit, and Twitter. OneGuy.org gives them a home you own."
- **Objection — "It's a .org, not a .com":** "Exactly. This isn't a commercial product — it's a movement brand. .org signals community and mission, which is what One Guy represents."
- **Objection — "The value is unclear for a .org":** "The value is in naming the category. 'One Guy' is what every AI-enabled solopreneur calls themselves. Owning that identity — even on .org — is owning the conversation."
