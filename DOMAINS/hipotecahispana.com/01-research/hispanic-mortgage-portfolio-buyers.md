## Portfolio Overview: The 4-Domain Ecosystem

| Domain | Role | Language | Search Intent | Buyer Fit |
|--------|------|----------|---------------|-----------|
| **HipotecaHispana.com** | Gateway / Brand | Spanish | "Hispanic mortgage" — trust, authority, education | Top of funnel: the brand that owns the category |
| **PagaTuCasa.com** | Action / Acquisition | Spanish (informal) | "Pay your house" — mortgage payment, loan application | Mid funnel: the CTA that converts browsers to borrowers |
| **PagaCasa.com** | Action / Short-form | Spanish (short) | "Pay house" — ultra-short, mobile-friendly | Bottom funnel: the 9-character action domain for ads/PPC |
| **PaguesuCasa.com** | Action / Professional | Spanish (formal) | "Pay your house" — financial services tone | Mid funnel: the formal/professional variant for bank branding |

**Portfolio Strategy:** One brand domain (HipotecaHispana) + three action domains (PagaTuCasa, PagaCasa, PaguesuCasa). Together they cover the entire Hispanic mortgage journey: discovery → trust → application → payment. Owning all four means owning every Spanish-language mortgage search variant that matters.

**💰 Portfolio Valuation Note:** Existing individual lead files anchor domains at $1K–$10K each (very conservative). As a strategic portfolio, the combined lot creates a competitive moat worth significantly more. A buyer acquiring all four locks competitors out of the top Spanish mortgage search terms. **Strategic portfolio value: $25K–$50K+** depending on buyer sophistication and defensive motivation.
