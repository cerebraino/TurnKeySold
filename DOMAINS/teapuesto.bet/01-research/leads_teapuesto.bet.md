## 1. Domain Valuation

**Concept:** "Te apuesto" = "I bet you" — the exact phrase Spanish speakers say when wagering with friends. Unlike "apuesto" (I bet, general), "te apuesto" is personal, direct, confrontational. Perfect for peer-to-peer social betting, influencer wagering challenges, and sports banter platforms. The .bet extension reinforces the gambling-specific use.

**Brandability:** 38/50 Tier 2 — Three words but a natural Spanish phrase. Personal. "Te apuesto" has emotional charge — it's what you say to a friend before a bet.
**Value:** $500–$3,000 | **Anchor:** $1,500
