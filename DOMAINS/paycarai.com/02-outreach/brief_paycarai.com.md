## Messaging Strategy
- **Primary Angle:** Enterprise Trust. paycarai.com is the .com version of the definitive car payments domain. For traditional financial institutions and auto lenders, .com signals legitimacy and permanence.
- **Secondary Angle:** SEO Dominance. .com has better ranking signals for exact-match keywords. paycarai.com captures organic search traffic that other domains miss.
- **Bundle Strategy:** Offer paycar.ai + paycarai.com as a package. .ai for the app/product brand; .com for redirect, SEO, and enterprise trust. Combined asking at a discount vs. separate purchases.
- **Key Talking Points:** 9 chars + .com (compact). "Pay Car AI" — clear, descriptive, modern. Enterprise-ready.
