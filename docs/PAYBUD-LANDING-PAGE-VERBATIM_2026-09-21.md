## Payquick.ai — "Speed wins. Get paid faster. Pay faster."
The instant payments brand. In a world where checkout abandonment spikes with every extra second of friction, speed isn't a feature — it's a revenue driver. Communicates the entire value proposition without a single additional word.

**Ideal for:** Real-time payout platforms · Payment orchestration · Checkout acceleration · Cross-border transfers · Embedded payments · Wallets & neobanks

## PayBud.ai — "Your payments buddy — smart, friendly, always on."
Hits the sweet spot between trust and approachability. Reads like a helpful AI companion living inside your product: splitting bills, sending reminders, coaching spending habits, or guiding users through a financial decision. In a space crowded with cold, transactional UX, PayBud feels warm.

**Ideal for:** Personal finance apps · AI budgeting tools · P2P & social payments · Neobanks adding AI · Payroll & earned wage access

## PayAuto.ai — "Payments on autopilot — set it, forget it, never miss."
Literal in the best way. The moment anyone reads it, they understand: automation, recurring billing, smart retries, scheduled transfers, collections workflows. A product feature packaged as a domain. For B2B fintech, that kind of clarity is worth more than a clever brand name.

**Ideal for:** AP/AR automation · Subscription billing · Invoicing & collections · Banks pushing autopay · Finance ops automation

## Paycar.ai — "A vertical brand for car payments — clean, direct, high intent."
Category-defining in the fastest-growing fintech vertical: automotive finance. Down payments, loan servicing, dealer financing flows, warranty portals, fleet billing, EV charging payments — massive, underserved UX moments. Owns the category in three syllables.

**Ideal for:** Online car marketplaces · Auto lenders & refinance · Dealer fintech · Fleet payments · EV charging & subscriptions

## Paybet.ai — "The funding step of wagering — one name that says it all."
Built for the highest-stakes, highest-friction moment in regulated gaming: getting money into the account, fast, cleanly, without a decline. Deposits drive revenue. Smooth deposits drive loyalty. Bold enough to be remembered.

**Ideal for:** Sportsbooks · iGaming operators · Regulated gaming wallets · High-risk payment processors · Gaming checkout optimization

## JogPay.com — "Payments in motion — fast, modern, mobile-first."
Has energy. Feels like something you'd tap, not type — perfectly positioned for mobile-first payment experiences, commuter/transit payments, gig economy payouts, or wearable fintech. The .com gives it mainstream gravity and long-term brand staying power.

**Ideal for:** Mobile payment apps · Tap-to-pay startups · Gig & creator payouts · Commuter & travel fintech · Wearable fintech

## PayDeal.ai — "Where every transaction closes a deal."
Fuses commerce and conversion into one confident brand. Signals negotiation, offers, and closing — the exact moment money changes hands. A name that promises buyers a win on every payment.

**Ideal for:** Marketplaces & deal platforms · BNPL & financing offers · **AI shopping & negotiation agents** · Dynamic pricing & checkout · B2B procurement & quotes
