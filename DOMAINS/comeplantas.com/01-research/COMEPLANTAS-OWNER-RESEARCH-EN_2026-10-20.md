### What the market context says (claims — UNVERIFIED)

- The money is thinning but concentrated: plant-based companies globally raised $450M in 2025 (GFI), and LatAm companies raised $261M in 2025 — down 52% from 2024 (Forwarding Fooding per source). Translation: fewer funded startups, but the survivors (NotCo, Heura, LIVEKINDLY) are the consolidation winners — exactly the ones that buy assets.
- The demand side is real: Mexico's plant-based meat market was $305.6M in 2025, projected to hit $1.68B by 2034 at 20%+ CAGR (IMARC), and Mexico startups raised $1.52B in the first three quarters of 2026 (Crunchbase).
- A key selling point the source claims verified: no major company currently owns the "Come Plantas" name — searches turned up only small social-media and apparel uses. That means no trademark giant is blocking a sale, but it also means the highest-payout scenario isn't a defensive purchase — it's a funded company adopting the name for a Spanish-language brand launch. Pitch it as a ready-made category name ("Eat Plants" in Spanish), not as resolving a conflict.

### How the source proposes to work the list

1. Direct outreach first — email NotCo's and Heura's brand/marketing leadership (a warm intro through LinkedIn beats a form). These two are the best combination of money + Spanish-market focus + recent positive momentum.
2. List simultaneously on Afternic and Sedo with a buy-it-now price — CPG brand teams often find domains there when a launch triggers urgency, and January (Veganuary) is the annual demand spike.
3. The source offered: drafting outreach email(s) to NotCo and Heura in Spanish, and/or researching current listed/comparable .com sale prices to help set the ask.
