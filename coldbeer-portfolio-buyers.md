### Insights
- **"Chela fría" is not just a search term — it's a cultural phrase** embedded in Mexican music, slang, and daily life. YouTube organic results are music videos and regional Mexican content, not e-commerce. This is a branding play, not just an SEO play.
- **Heineken México leads paid** — Tecate's entire brand identity is built around "cold beer" for working-class Mexicans. ChelaFria.com is Tecate's slogan in a domain.
- **Delivery apps (Rappi, Uber Eats) dominate organic** — they capture "chela fria" intent and monetize through delivery. A beer brand owning ChelaFria.com could redirect to their own DTC delivery (Zé Delivery/TaDa for AB InBev) or partner with delivery platforms.
- **Zé Delivery (AB InBev)** is the most natural buyer — they already deliver "chela fria" on-demand in Brazil and are expanding across LATAM. ChelaFria.com could be their Mexican market entry domain.
- The phrase "chela fría" generates significant type-in traffic that currently goes to search engines. An exact-match domain captures this intent before it reaches Google/Bing.
- **DMPS bonus:** AB InBev (+10), Heineken (+10). The two dominant beer companies already compete for "chela fría" in both paid and organic.
