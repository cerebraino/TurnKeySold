## 3. Industry Angle (updated with Genspark insight)

"Vive Mucho" is a warm, aspirational Spanish phrase — "live a lot, live well, live fully." It works for fitness, wellness, travel, lifestyle — and, per the Genspark research, **longevity medicine** ("vivir muchos años"). The imperative "vive" (live!) is a call to action; "mucho" (a lot) is the promise. Best buyers: (1) longevity/wellness clinics, doctors and supplement brands with Spanish-speaking audiences (35 verified prospects in the Genspark list), and (2) fitness/wellness apps expanding into Spanish-language markets.

**Double meaning to sell (Genspark pitch, point 3):** longevidad ("vivir muchos años") + vida plena ("vivir mucho, intensamente").
