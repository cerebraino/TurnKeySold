## Positioning
NYC has 2.5 million Hispanic residents — the largest of any US city. These two domains capture the complete NYC Hispanic search landscape: "Hispanic" for bilingual audiences, "Hispano" for Spanish-preferred audiences. Together, they own every NYC Hispanic search variant. For the NYC Hispanic Chamber of Commerce or a major media outlet, these domains ARE their digital identity.
