## The Dual Meaning — Apuesto's Secret Weapon

**"Apuesto" has TWO meanings in Spanish:**

1. **"I bet"** (verb aposta) — gambling, wagering, risk-taking. The primary use case.
2. **"Handsome / elegant"** (adjective) — stylish, well-dressed, refined. A compliment.

This dual meaning is extraordinarily rare and valuable:
- **Betting brands** get a name that also means "elegant" — positioning them as the sophisticated, premium choice
- **Luxury brands** get a name that means "I bet" — edgy, risk-taking, confident
- The word itself bridges gambling culture and luxury lifestyle — exactly the Vegas/Monaco/Macau aesthetic
