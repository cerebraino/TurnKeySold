**Question asked:** what's feasible realistic sell price for each type buyer?

**The realistic price picture** — grounded in 2025 aftermarket data, not the headline million-dollar sales. The honest framing: ComePlantas.com is a low-value domain to investors but a high-value one to the right end-user, which is exactly the profile worth holding out for.

### Why these numbers

The domain's tier: "ComePlantas" is a short two-word .com (11 characters, no hyphens), which puts its generic value in the $1,500–$3,000 band. But the positive adjusters stack up: highly brandable (+25–40%), easy to spell in Spanish (+10–15%), growing niche (+15–30% — Mexico's plant-based market alone is growing 20%+ CAGR per the IMARC report cited earlier). That's how a funded end-user pushes it into five figures.

The negative adjuster that caps investor value: it's a Spanish phrase, so the global investor pool mostly won't get it — and English speakers misread "come." This is precisely why an investor will pay $500 while a Mexican or Spanish brand pays 20x that: the end-user pays for the fit, the investor pays a fraction.

The realistic expectation: properly priced domains sell in 6–18 months; overpriced ones sit 5–10 years (DomainDetails). A $100k+ outcome is real but is the tail, not the base case — roughly 5% of all sales reach it.

### How the source would set it up

- Buy-it-now at $25,000 with a private floor of ~$7,500 on Afternic and Sedo — the ask leaves negotiation room while staying inside the range a funded startup's marketing budget clears without a committee.
- Don't take the first flipper offer — a $1,000 wholesale offer means nothing compared to one Veganuary cycle of waiting; list by early December so the January demand spike hits your lander.
- For the NotCo/Heura-tier direct outreach, anchor at $30,000 and settle around $10k–$20k; for a CPG or brand house lead, route through a domain broker (they expect it) with a valuation report in hand.
- To pin the comps tighter before you commit to a price, run "vegan," "plantbased," and "comer" filters on NameBio (last 2 years, $1,000+ only) and a free GoDaddy appraisal as a sanity floor — treat the tools as a floor, never the ceiling, for a name like this.

**Bottom line (source's):** expect $2,000–$10,000 in a normal sale, aim for $10,000–$30,000 from a funded Spanish-market player, and treat anything above $50,000 as the lottery ticket you don't wait for.
