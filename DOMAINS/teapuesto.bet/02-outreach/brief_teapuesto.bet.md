- **Domain:** teapuesto.bet
- **Niche:** Social Betting / Peer-to-Peer Wagering / Spanish-Language Gambling

## Tone & Cultural Considerations
- **Tone:** Direct, energetic, social. This domain has emotional charge — it's what friends say. The messaging should match that energy: confident, playful, human.
- **Language:** Bilingual. English for global operators (Bet365, DraftKings); Spanish for LATAM-native operators (Caliente, Codere).
- **Cultural Context:** "Te apuesto" carries more emotional weight than "I bet you" in English — it's more personal, more confrontational, more fun. Spanish speakers use it constantly in social contexts.
- **Objection — "We already have a brand":** "This isn't about replacing your brand. teapuesto.bet is a social betting vertical — the domain for your peer-to-peer features, influencer challenges, and friend wagers. It complements your core brand."
- **Objection — "Too niche / low value":** "It's available now — and it's the exact phrase millions of Spanish speakers use daily. For the cost of a small PPC campaign, you own the social betting phrase in Spanish. All three extensions together cover sports, casino, and web3."
