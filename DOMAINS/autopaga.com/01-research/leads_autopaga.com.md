## 1. Domain Valuation Assessment

### Positioning
**autopaga.com = "Auto Paga"** (Spanish: *paga automáticamente* / "pay automatically"). The Spanish/LATAM domain for **agentic payments** — AI agents and automated systems that issue payments on behalf of users. Twin of **payauto.ai** ("Pay Automatically" + AI). Together they form a bilingual bundle: the agentic payments category name in English and Spanish.

### The Trend (early-stage, moving fast — LATAM angle)
- Agentic payments are going global; LATAM fintechs are among the fastest adopters of AI payments (Mercado Pago has 300M+ ecosystem users; Clip/Kushki/dLocal are regional rails).
- "autopaga" reads as **recurring/auto-pay AND agent-initiated payments** — a natural fit for LATAM's subscription economy (streaming, fintech apps, BNPL installments).
- The .com + Spanish compound is instantly understood across Mexico, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, Peru, Spain and the US Hispanic market.

## 3. Bundle Strategy (autopaga.com + payauto.ai)

- **autopaga.com** = Spanish/LATAM: Mercado Pago, Clip, Kushki, dLocal, Nubank, Pomelo, EBANX, Yuno, Belvo.
- **payauto.ai** = English/global: Stripe, Visa, Mastercard, Payman, Perplexity, PayPal.
- **Pair logic:** "Pay automatically" in both languages — the agentic payments category name for every market. Buyer who wants LATAM on-ramp also wants the global .ai flagship.
- **Sequence:** lead with autopaga.com for LATAM buyers; offer payauto.ai as the global extension of the same infrastructure. Lead with payauto.ai for global buyers; offer autopaga.com as the LATAM on-ramp.
