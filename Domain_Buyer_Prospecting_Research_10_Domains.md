# 1. Research Objective

The goal of this research is not simply to identify companies related to each domain name.

The objective is to find buyers who combine:

- **Strong strategic fit**
- **Observable urgency or timing**
- **A meaningful domain-upgrade opportunity**
- **Ability to pay**
- **Strategic category or brand value**
- **Reachable decision-makers**
- **Potential competitive or defensive pressure**

The operating principle is:

> **Strategic Fit × Ability to Pay × Trigger/Timing × Reachability = Prospect Quality**

The ten domains selected for this test were:

1. NoBreak.ai
2. NoFail.ai
3. Automovil.ai
4. KnowLaw.ai
5. PayCar.ai
6. HipotecaHispana.com

# 2. Scoring Framework

## Buyer Motivation Score (BMS)

Each buyer is scored from 0–100 based on:

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Strategic domain fit | 25 |
| Trigger / timing | 20 |
| Domain-upgrade need | 15 |
| Ability to pay | 15 |
| Brand/category upside | 10 |
| Decision-maker reachability | 10 |
| Competitive/defensive pressure | 5 |

### Interpretation

- **85–100:** Exceptional — immediate personalized outreach
- **70–84:** Strong — high priority
- **55–69:** Plausible — second wave / enrich first
- **Below 55:** Weak — generally exclude

## Strategic Value Multiplier (SVM)

Estimated willingness-to-pay / strategic importance:

- **5 — Transformational**
- **4 — High**
- **3 — Moderate**
- **2 — Low**
- **1 — Speculative**

A high BMS does not automatically imply a high price ceiling.

A prospect can be:
- highly motivated but financially weak,
- strategically valuable but poorly timed,
- or both highly motivated and highly valuable.

# 6. Most Important Strategic Findings

## Finding 1 — Exact-brand upgrades are powerful, but not always highest-value

Examples:

- NoBreak Security → NoBreak.ca → NoBreak.ai
- KnowLaw AI → knowlawai.com → KnowLaw.ai

These are extremely easy strategic narratives.

However, the buyer's ability to pay still matters.

KnowLaw.ai may have a perfect brand match but a lower financial ceiling.

---

## Finding 2 — Trigger-based prospecting is more valuable than static similarity

The strongest opportunities came from recent events:

- Supreme Lending launching Supreme CASA
- Zócalo Health raising capital
- Sazabi emerging from stealth and raising funding
- Kavak raising major capital and expanding AI use
- Taskade publishing directly about one-person companies
- NoBreak actively acquiring founding households

This is exactly what the Buyer Prospector Skill should prioritize.

---

## Finding 3 — High-LTV conventional industries may outperform fashionable AI buyers

HipotecaHispana.com is potentially more commercially valuable than several `.ai` names because mortgage customer economics are extremely large.

Similarly:

- LatinoMedico.com
- HispanoAbogado.com

map to customer-acquisition markets where a strategic domain can have measurable economic value.

---

## Finding 4 — Some domains have a single dominant buyer; others should be auctioned strategically through outbound

### Single-dominant-buyer style

- NoBreak.ai
- KnowLaw.ai

### Competitive-buyer style

- NoFail.ai
- HipotecaHispana.com
- LatinoMedico.com
- Automovil.ai
- PayCar.ai

The sales strategy should differ accordingly.
