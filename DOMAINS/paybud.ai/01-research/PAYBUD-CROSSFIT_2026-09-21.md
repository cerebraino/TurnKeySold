## Positioning difference (why overlap is partial, not total)
- **payauto.ai** = "Pay Automatically" — the *infrastructure/category* name for agentic payments. Buyers: payments rails, authorization, tokenization, checkout execution.
- **paybud.ai** = "Pay + Bud" (buddy/budget) — the *friendly consumer* name. "Your AI payment buddy." Buyers: consumer wallets, budgeting apps, neobanks, agent wallets with a consumer face, P2P social payments ("pay your bud").
