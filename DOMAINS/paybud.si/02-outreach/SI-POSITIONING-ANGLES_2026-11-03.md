## §2 Positioning under the owner reframe

**One-line:** *the `.ai` and the `.si` of the same pay-brand leave together, so the one-keystroke twin never ends up in a stranger's hands.*

- **Not Slovenia, and not even primarily a `.si` sale.** The owner reframe (global SI trend) gives this asset its market, but the **asset logic is pair-completion**: `paybud.ai` is ours (expires Dec 15 2027, auto-renew ON) and `paybud.com` is an anonymous GoDaddy registration from 2011 that serves nothing. There is **no incumbent to fight** — which lowers the standalone buyer count but makes the pair clean.
- **The brand-protection reframe, applied honestly:** we are not telling a prospect that someone is squatting their name — nobody owns `paybud` as a brand we can identify. We are telling them that **the twin is the likeliest typo of the `.ai`**, and that in this pair both legs are already under one owner, so a buyer takes the typo problem off the table at the same time as the brand. That is traceable (QWERTY adjacency, Netcraft; >44,000 names exist in both `.ai` and `.si`, ~22% of all `.si`) and it does not require inventing a threat.
- **Why `.si` at all:** for an EU-facing pay product, `.si` is a European ccTLD that now carries a public "super intelligence" reading — an SI marker for a pay-brand, not a Slovenian one.
- **Comp context (market context only, never a valuation):** compound band **$2,000–$5,000**; `X402.si`+`QA.si` **$7,839** (agent-payments protocol term, sold as a pair — directly relevant to a pair logic). No price is set or implied here; the owner sets any ladder, and `paybud.ai`'s own anchor lives in the payments-family plan, not in this file.
